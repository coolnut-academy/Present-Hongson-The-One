# Product brief: HONGSON THE ONE Immersive Exhibit

## Core proposition
The HONGSON THE ONE project at โรงเรียนห้องสอนศึกษา ในพระอุปถัมภ์ฯ (Mae Hong Son) is a **Super App & Local Digital Education Ecosystem**. It organizes access to teacher/student digital services and supports a continuous improvement loop: **WORK → LEARN → CREATE → SHARE**. Its flagship product is **HONGSON T-Folio**, a teacher digital portfolio and performance management web app with the **4R** workflow (Record, Review, Retrieve, Report).

This deliverable is a **standalone, cinematic public presentation website** using the school's authentic logos, product screenshots, local identity and real evidence. HONGSON THE ONE has its own 27-image presentation/evidence deck in `reference-private/hongson-one-deck/`, separate from the 17-image T-Folio slideshow. It must be compelling enough to replace a traditional slide deck for 3–10 minute talks while still working for self-directed audiences.

## Audiences
1. Evaluation panels, educational innovators, provincial education staff.
2. Teachers/administrators seeking to understand use cases.
3. Visitors browsing online, many on mobile.

## Design goals
- 1–3 seconds to understand what HONGSON THE ONE is.
- Distinguish Super App ecosystem vs flagship T-Folio clearly.
- Show product substance: workflows, screens, approval process, realistic screenshots and source documentation.
- Make viewers feel cinematic, not visually overwhelmed; the 3D environment serves the story.
- Presentation mode: one controlled chapter at a time; Explore mode: natural vertical scroll.
- No form/login/payment, no server. Static GitHub Pages hosting.

## Avoid
- Making a full working T-Folio clone; this is a showcase, not an operational system.
- Conflating ecosystem with a unified SSO/database.
- Turning every section into the same glass card grid.
- Publishing raw sensitive report/slides without reviewing for privacy.
- Making unsupported quantified efficiency claims (e.g., "ลดเอกสาร 90%", "ครูทุกคนใช้งาน 100%").

## Success signals
A presenter can navigate the entire story with keyboard/buttons; a mobile visitor can scroll forward/backward with visible 3D movement; a low-end phone gets a comprehensible reduced-quality scene instead of a broken blank canvas; facts are sourced; GH Pages link works after push.
