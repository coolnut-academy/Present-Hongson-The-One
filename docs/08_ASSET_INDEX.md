## Public screenshot workflow
1. Select 3–5 real screenshots that show app **without personal data**.
2. Rebuild or redact private parts with neutral masks (don't falsify KPIs).
3. Export responsive WebP/AVIF to `public/hongson-assets/screenshots/` with clear filenames.
4. Maintain attribution to slide numbers in evidence drawer.

## External media
Do not automatically pull unsourced AI imagery into production. If generating/crafting decorative 3D, label it "conceptual visualization", and do not invent the school's real UI, people, building or verified outcomes.
