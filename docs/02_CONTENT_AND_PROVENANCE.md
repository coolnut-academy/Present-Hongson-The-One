- 4R process **Record → Review → Retrieve → Report** (A p. 22).

- Administrator workflow **User Management → Category Config → Compliance → Approval → KPI Dashboard → Backup** (B slide 06).
- Approval flow **Teacher Submit → Deputy Review → Director Approve → Complete**, with comments/revision when necessary (B slide 07).

Screenshots guide B slides 12–17 show login/dashboard/new portfolio/print/analytics. Treat these as presented product capabilities; verify against live system before claiming detailed capabilities beyond deck.
- Technical slide depicts Next.js, Firebase Auth, Firestore, Storage, Vercel (B slide 09). This is a slide description, not audited production-stack confirmation.
- A reports school-level deployment context, but independently audited usage totals, saved staff hours and efficiency before-after metrics are NOT present; do not invent them.
- Slide 11 says "Expected Impact" (expected, not measured!). Preserve expected-vs-achieved distinction.

## HONGSON METAVERSE MODEL — separate evidenced innovation
- Roblox-based digital museum for culture/learning covering 7 districts (A pp. 4, 11, 26–29).
- 50 student leaders; 8 local experts, 32 integrated learning units (A p. 4).
- 485 student sample; average local knowledge score **12.85 → 16.37 / 20** after using Metaverse (A pp. 4 and 29). Observational report, do not claim causal proof.
- Reported satisfaction **92.4%**, scoped to Metaverse project, not the whole Super App (A pp. 4, 29).
- Reported academic dissemination at 9 venues and study visits from 16 groups (A pp. 19–20). Do not infer adoption outcomes.
